Introduction

Sita Sharma is a visionary entrepreneur with over 12 years of experience in fine jewelry design and luxury retail. She holds an MBA from a leading business school and has grown Anemora from a small boutique into a nationally recognized brand,once achieving an impressive 8x increase in annual revenue, while adopting eco-friendly H2O-based polishing techniques for sustainable jewelry care. Known for her elegant and detail-oriented approach to craftsmanship, she continues to inspire her team to blend tradition with modern design every single day.
